Detailed Journey & Capabilities

My tech career began at the physical hardware layer. As the founder of ALSTECH, I built expertise in laptop and phone repairs, hardware diagnostics, system optimization, software configuration, and precision microsoldering for delicate motherboard circuitry.

Expanding into software development, I mastered frontend engineering building ultra fast, responsive web experiences using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Next.js, and TypeScript. In the Web3 ecosystem, I found my passion for building decentralized products, wallet integrations, and founding Web3Central (a platform indexing 400+ Web3 tools).

Beyond coding and hardware, I drive digital presence through Social Media Management (specializing in Instagram, X, and Facebook growth, engagement strategies, and ad campaign creation), while continuously refining motion design animations using After Effects and CapCut.
